I am currently a lecturer at the School of Computer Science and Information Engineering, Hefei University of Technology (HFUT). I received my Ph.D. from South China University of Technology (SCUT) in June 2025. From 2023 to 2024, I was a CSC-sponsored visiting Ph.D. student at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. My recent research interests focus on 3D computer vision, scene reconstruction, multi-agent systems, and spatial intelligence. Over the past five years, I have published over 20 papers in prestigious venues, including leading journals (IJCV, TIP, TVCG, TMM, TCSVT, TITS) and top-tier conferences (ICCV, ACM MM). I am also a co-inventor of three granted patents. I serve on the Technical Program Committee for Pacific Graphics (PG) 2026. I also serve as a reviewer for leading journals such as IEEE TIP, TVCG, TMM, TCSVT, and TITS, as well as premier conferences like AAAI and ACM MM.

Openings
